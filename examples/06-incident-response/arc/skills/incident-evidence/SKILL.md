# Incident Evidence Procedure

1. Assign stable identifiers to evidence without modifying originals.
2. Record timestamps, source, collection method, and cryptographic digest.
3. Keep facts, hypotheses, and decisions in separate sections.
4. Never clear logs or history as a remediation step.
5. Close with containment actions, unresolved risks, and follow-up owners.
